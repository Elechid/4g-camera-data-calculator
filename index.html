How much SIM data does one 4G camera use in 30 days?

At 2 Mbps, 10 minutes of remote viewing per day uses about 4.5 GB over 30 days. Separate uploads at 1 Mbps for 5 minutes daily add 1.125 GB. That gives 5.625 GB, or 6.75 GB with 20% headroom. Local microSD recording alone uses no mobile data. Compare the estimate with the first week’s SIM usage.
